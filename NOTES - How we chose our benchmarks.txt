Benchmark Selection Methodology – Working Notes

Problem

Canadian Tire is difficult to benchmark because it is not a pure-play retailer.

The company operates across multiple business lines:

* General merchandise retail
* Automotive retail
* Sporting goods and outdoor products
* Home products and hardware
* Financial services (Canadian Tire Financial Services)
* Real estate exposure through CT REIT

As a result, no single company provides a perfect comparison.

This creates a benchmarking challenge that should be acknowledged explicitly in the report rather than ignored.

⸻

Source Used

S&P Capital IQ was used as the primary source for identifying comparable companies.

Two relevant competitor sources were available:

Competitor List

Canadian Tire’s competitor page contained approximately 37 competitors.

This list included:

* Walmart
* Amazon
* Costco
* Sobeys
* Metro
* Loblaw
* Home Depot
* IKEA
* Nordstrom
* PetSmart
* Wayfair
* Cabela’s
* Home Hardware
* Leon’s
* others

This list appears to be broad and strategic in nature.

It identifies companies that compete with Canadian Tire in some aspect of its business, but not necessarily companies that are suitable financial benchmarks.

Example:

* Amazon competes in e-commerce.
* Home Depot competes in home improvement.
* PetSmart competes in pet products.
* Sobeys competes indirectly through household spending budgets.

These companies are competitors but not necessarily good financial comparables.

⸻

Capital IQ Quick Comps

Capital IQ’s Quick Comps feature identified the following closest publicly traded comparable companies:

* Metro
* Dollarama
* Empire (Sobeys)
* Loblaw
* Wesfarmers
* Amazon
* several international retailers

These companies appear to be selected based on business similarity, financial characteristics, and valuation comparability rather than simply competitive overlap.

For accounting analysis purposes, the Quick Comp list is considered more reliable than the broader competitor list.

⸻

Selected Industry Benchmark Group

The benchmark group will consist of:

* Metro
* Dollarama
* Empire (Sobeys)
* Loblaw

⸻

Why These Companies Were Selected

Metro

* Large Canadian retailer
* Publicly traded
* IFRS reporting
* Significant food and household goods retail presence
* Similar exposure to Canadian consumer spending

⸻

Empire (Sobeys)

* Large Canadian retailer
* Publicly traded
* IFRS reporting
* National retail footprint
* Similar size to Canadian Tire
* Already identified as a useful comparable through prior analysis

Empire is likely the strongest candidate for the “most comparable company” requirement.

⸻

Loblaw

* Largest Canadian retailer in the group
* Publicly traded
* IFRS reporting
* Significant exposure to consumer spending
* Major national retail network

Provides a useful upper-scale benchmark.

⸻

Dollarama

* Publicly traded
* Canadian retailer
* Significant national footprint
* Consumer discretionary exposure
* Included by Capital IQ in its peer group

Provides additional perspective from a different but still relevant retail model.

⸻

Why Other Companies Were Excluded

Walmart

Not included in industry averages.

Reasons:

* Global operations
* Much larger scale
* Different operating model
* Different competitive environment

However, Walmart remains useful as a strategic comparison company because it represents a highly successful large-scale retailer and provides context for operational efficiency and strategic direction.

Use:

* Strategic comparator

Not use:

* Industry benchmark calculations

⸻

Amazon

Excluded because:

* Technology platform characteristics
* Marketplace business model
* Cloud computing operations (AWS)
* Global scale vastly exceeds Canadian Tire

Poor accounting benchmark despite being a competitive threat.

⸻

Costco

Excluded because:

* Membership warehouse model
* Different pricing and inventory strategy
* Different margin structure

Not directly comparable.

⸻

Home Depot

Excluded because:

* Pure home improvement retailer
* Narrower business model
* No financial services segment

⸻

IKEA, Wayfair, Nordstrom, PetSmart, etc.

Excluded because:

* Too specialised
* Not representative of Canadian Tire’s overall business model

⸻

Most Comparable Company Selection

Selected company:

Empire (Sobeys)

Reasons:

* Canadian company
* IFRS reporting
* Similar scale
* Similar market environment
* National retail presence
* Similar maturity profile
* Appears in Capital IQ peer group
* Strong availability of financial data

No company perfectly matches Canadian Tire.

Empire is not a perfect peer but represents the most defensible single-company comparison available.

Key Point for Report

A major conclusion from the benchmarking process is that Canadian Tire lacks a truly perfect comparable company because of its combination of:

* Retail operations
* Financial services
* Real estate interests

Rather than weakening the analysis, this justifies the use of multiple benchmarking approaches (historical, peer, industry, and strategic comparisons) to develop a more reliable assessment of long-term financial health and investment attractiveness.
