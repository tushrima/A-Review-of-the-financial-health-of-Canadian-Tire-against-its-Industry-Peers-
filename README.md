Report Scenario & Requirements
For this group project, you will be sorted into groups and required to select one public company. This company should be listed and publicly traded on a stock exchange. This company may use International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) or US GAAP in the preparation of its financial statements. The company cannot be a bank, insurance company, or a trust.

Your group will assume the role of an investment advisor working on a project for a client investment firm. You are required to analyse the company that you have chosen and submit a report to the CEO of the investment firm. You are expected to advise the CEO (me) on the desirability of making a long-term investment in the chosen company. The CEO has specifically asked you to evaluate the long-term financial health of the chosen company utilizing reliable accounting information available on the company and make a clear recommendation on whether the investment firm should or should not invest in the company.

For your report, you should select appropriate ratios that will provide the information you need to carry out your analysis. You are expected to compare the financial position and performance of your chosen company as follows:

over time,
against the industry in which the company operates, and
against an appropriately determined most comparable company.
The CEO wants to emphasize that any comparison is meaningful only if there is a credible and reliable benchmark against which the comparison can be made. To help the CEO decide whether or not he can rely on the comparisons that you make, you should explain in your report how you derive the industry norms and why you think the benchmarks used in your comparisons are appropriate. The CEO specifically requests that you disclose the names of the companies in your industry group and explain why it is appropriate to include these specific companies and only these companies in the calculation of your industry norms.

Note

The CEO is aware of the possibility that different companies may not be using the same accounting policy to account for the same item because accounting policy choices are allowed in many areas under IFRS. One topic the CEO has recently read about is the choice available to companies under IFRS in using either historical cost or market value in the measurement of the property, plant and equipment assets. The CEO is interested in the topic and specifically requests that you review the property, plant & equipment note for the company you've selected and its industry competitors and include some discussions in your report regarding how this impacts your analysis.

Additional Requirements

The CEO also wants to see in your report:

Some reference to the current situation of your company as depicted in recent news reports

Strong visuals, particularly if they were made in Tableau or Power BI (or to a lesser extent, Excel)

An explanation of whether you used generative AI in the report, and if so, how it was used. He is happy to see it used to enhance the report, but does not want to see it perform the bulk of the work

Tip

Remember that the CEO is very busy. Therefore, it is important to be clear and concise. The body of your report should include any tables necessary to the understanding of your report, but any calculations or background work associated with the tables should be in an appendix. The CEO expects you to know the importance of proofreading all formal reports and reminds you that typos, misprints, grammatical errors, and a lack of attention to detail can significantly undermine your professional image and the credibility of your report.

Formatting & Content
Document Format Requirements

To be accepted, the written report must be prepared electronically in Microsoft Word document format. The report should be formatted as follows:

Font: 12-point Times New Roman

Page Size: 21.59 x 27.94 cm (or 8½ x 11 inches) letter page size

Spacing: Double-spaced body text

Margins: At least 2.54 cm (or one inch) on all sides

Line Limit: No more than 23 lines on a normal page

Citations: All items of within-text citations, footnotes (if there are any), and the reference list must follow the APA style

Tip

New to APA formatting? No problem! Check out this APA style guideDownload APA style guide, which shows you full application of APA styling.

Required Report Components

The report should include the following sections:

Title Page
This page will have the title of your report, your name(s), a date (submission date) and the course number/name.
Abstract Page
This single page follows the title page. In one paragraph of 150 words or less, this abstract will summarize your entire project and provide concise descriptions of your purpose, methodology, findings/conclusions, and recommendation to the CEO.
Body of the Paper
Your report must have an introduction and conclusion. Exclusive of the title page, abstract, reference list, and the appendices, the body of the paper is not to exceed 10 pages. This does not mean that you have to write 10 pages.
List of References
Note that the reference list is not a bibliography! Textbooks are not to be used as references in the writing of the report.
Appendices
All appendices follow the list of references. All your detailed calculations, if any, should be included in the appendices and not in the body of the paper.
Note

Tables and figures that you include as part of the body of the paper are counted towards the 10-page limit. If you have any footnotes, they can be placed either at the bottom of the page in the body of the text or at the end of the text (called endnotes). Use footnotes or endnotes sparingly. All footnotes/endnotes are part of the body of the paper and therefore are also counted towards the 10-page limit.

Tip

If you are using appendices, be sure that their existence is referenced in the body of the paper so that the CEO knows where to look for more detailed information. Do not include in these appendices anything that is not referenced in the body of the paper. Everything important such as tables and graphs that you want the CEO to read should be in the body of the paper and not the appendices. The appendices are used for supplementary material and non-essential information only. Tables, graphs, and similar items that you put in these appendices will not be graded for additional marks.

Note

A company is singular. Pronoun references to a company must use 'it' and not 'they'.
